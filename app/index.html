About and audio credits

This offline trainer uses human Mandarin recordings from the CC-BY-SA audio-cmn corpus, definitions from CC BY-SA CC-CEDICT, and public-domain pinyin audio for tone comparisons. Practice uses acoustic screening or explicit listening approval for the exact recordings and labels. Automatic screening is not a guarantee of accuracy.

Mandarin Native: external listening reference (online). Checked direct word recordings are also available in the local-use app. Sentence audio is not used for practice; local-use imports are excluded from redistributable builds.
